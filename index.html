Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Shelter Secure?

Shelter Secure provides education and personalized household reporting to help you understand your home's essential dependencies, financial picture, and opportunities to improve readiness and continuity.

Do I need to own a home?

No. Shelter Secure is for homeowners and renters, in town or rural. Your bonus report can even be used for a property you're interested in, or given to someone else.

What's the difference between Grid-Adjacent and the Shelter Report System?

The Grid-Adjacent course ($67) teaches the ideas: how energy, water, food, shelter and money work together in a household. It includes six guided sections, a workbook and audio, and the full-color digital book. The Shelter Report System ($179) applies those ideas to your own household with personalized reports and plans. You can buy either one on its own, or both together for $199.

Can I update my information later?

Yes. Come back whenever life changes, update your answers and generate a fresh report. Earlier reports stay in your library so you can see your progress.

Will someone inspect my home?

No. The Shelter Report System is automated and educational, based on the information you provide. It is not an inspection, an appraisal, or financial, lending, insurance or legal advice. Professional consultation is separate and not included.

What happens after I purchase?

Checkout is handled securely through SamCart. For the course, your access link arrives in your purchase email. For the Shelter Report System, you receive an email invitation to set your own password, then sign in at app.sheltersecurenow.com and start with Quick View. A free video instruction manual walks you through each section.

What if it isn't right for me?

Every purchase comes with a 14-day guarantee. If the course, the Shelter Report System or the bundle isn't right for you, email support@sheltersecurenow.com within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. See the full Refund Policy.

Is my information private?

Your answers belong to your account alone. Labels like "Savings One" are all the portal needs: no account numbers, employer or bank names, or documents. Estimates are fine. We do not sell your information. Read our Privacy Policy.

When will the Continuity Passport, Document Seal and Title Integrity Guard be available?

All three are planned for 2027, and their features are still being finalized. Sign up above and we'll let you know when each one opens.
